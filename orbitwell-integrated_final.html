📋 Onboard Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Test Protocols

Follow these standardized NASA / CSA ISS clinical measurement rules to prevent microgravity fluid-shift artifacts and sensor calibration errors.

1. Radiation Dose Tracking (CSA CANS / Dosimeters)

  1. Personal Dosimeter Placement: Wear passive/active dosimeter badges continuously on the chest pocket; never store inside shielded sleep quarters during EVA.
  2. Neutron Spectrometer Sync: Sync the Canadian Active Neutron Spectrometer (CANS) telemetry prior to sleep cycle every 24 hours.
  3. Threshold Rule: Any neutron spike ≥ 0.5 mSv/hr or daily dose ≥ 5.0 mSv triggers immediate Solar Particle Event (SPE) storm-shelter protocol.

2. Pulmonary & Spirometry Testing (CHeCS)

  1. Zero-G Posture Rule: Anchor feet into foot restraints with torso upright; microgravity elevates the diaphragm, slightly lowering baseline FVC by ~5–8%.
  2. Calibration: Perform a 3-liter syringe flow calibration check before inserting a sterile single-use bacterial filter mouthpiece.
  3. Maneuver: Inhale maximally, seal lips tightly, and blast air out forcefully for at least 6 seconds. Repeat 3 times and log the highest FEV1/FVC ratio.

3. Microfluidic Blood Analyzer (1DROP / i-STAT)

  1. Surface Tension Rule: In microgravity, blood beads rather than dripping. Warm finger for 60 seconds, lance lateral fingertip, and touch microfluidic cartridge inlet directly to the blood dome.
  2. Bubble Prevention: Ensure capillary channel fills completely without air gaps; air bubbles invalidate WBC and electrolyte readings.
  3. Disposal: Seal used lancets and bio-cartridges immediately in the sharp-containment Biohazard Bag.

4. Metabolic & Nutritional Screening

  1. Fasting Window: Conduct serum ferritin, albumin, and Vitamin D spot checks after an 8-hour overnight fast (water permitted).
  2. Hemolysis Avoidance: Do not squeeze the finger excessively during capillary draw, which ruptures red cells and artificially elevates potassium/ferritin.
  3. Supplementation Rule: Log daily Vitamin D3 (800–1000 IU) intake alongside assay results.

5. Urine Analysis Test (Urolax Strip & 24h Pool)

  1. Microgravity Collection: Use the airflow-assisted Urine Collection Device (UCD) sleeve to prevent liquid escape.
  2. Dipstick Timing: Wet all reagent pads on the Urolax test strip, blot edge gently on lint-free wipe, and insert into the optical reader at exactly 60 seconds.
  3. Stone Prevention Rule: Specific Gravity > 1.025 or Urinary Calcium > 250 mg/24h indicates concentrated urine and high nephrolithiasis risk.

6. Sleep-Wake Actigraphy (Actiwatch Spectrum)

  1. Sensor Wear Rule: Wear the wrist actigraph on the non-dominant wrist 24/7, keeping the ambient light sensor uncovered by flight suit sleeves.
  2. Rest-Activity Logging: Press the event marker button when entering sleep restraints ("lights out") and immediately upon waking.
  3. Circadian Rule: Orbital sunrise occurs every 90 minutes; use blue-enriched LED lighting during wake shifts and dim amber lighting 2 hours pre-sleep.
